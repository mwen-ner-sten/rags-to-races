# County Baton workshop

**Decision.** Retire County Baton. Keep freight as an experimental choice in Salvage carryover. A River player taking a worn River Tire to Quarry can leave it behind, give up a perk slot to carry it, or spend 8 Legacy Points to keep both the tire and the slot. The same choice applies to a Quarry Four going to River.

## System admission test

| Charter question | Two-leg baton | Freight inside salvage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. What decision? | Accept a prescribed kart, part, race, destination and Beater, or skip the attempt. | Weigh this part’s condition and availability at the destination against a perk slot and 8 LP. |
| 2. Why repeat? | The reverse route earned a second permit. Later deliveries repeated a solved build. | A fresh native part, a different perk loadout or a planned purchase changes the price worth paying. An established garage might always pay. |
| 3. Which pillar? | Junk Becomes Identity gave one tire a history across two cars. | Resets Change Strategy. A River tire can shape a Quarry opening where tire sourcing is slower. Its provenance and condition travel with it. |
| 4. What cost? | Two Parts With a Past clears, a passport, two objective Seasons, a slot and attention to a particular UID. | One optional checkbox, one part, and either one slot for the Season or 8 LP upfront. |
| 5. When? | After the passport and second clear. | Once ordinary carryover and county travel are earned, when an eligible part and destination are selected. Experimental careers must be on to buy freight. |
| 6. When automated? | Race settlement awarded proof. The player managed protection and transfer. | The player chooses once at reset. Settlement handles the fee and delivery. |
| 7. What resets? | Scrap Reset continued or ended the attempt. Founding or a Shop Move ended it. Earned permits lived in meta. | Only Scrap Reset ships a part. It can cross once. Team Reset and Shop Move clear the physical garage. No permit ledger. |
| 8. Evidence of improvement? | Six automated routes delivered the right UID. They supplied no evidence of players wanting another attempt. | Give players a tight LP budget and then a crowded perk loadout. Ask them to predict the opening and choose unaided. Look for a reason to switch payment and evidence they actually fit the part. |
| 9. What labor is compressed? | Mostly tracking the qualifying part once the player could find the safeguard. | Carryover already avoids re-sourcing one component. Freight buys back the slot for a chosen opening perk. |
| 10. New responsibility? | Keep a single component alive across two Seasons. | Budget a county move in the existing reset plan. No extra career to manage. |
| 11. Casual default and expert depth? | Skipping was easy. Continuing required understanding several exceptions. | Default to no part. Selecting a part defaults to the crate. An expert can price a particular slot against their next perk purchase. |

## Why cut the challenge?

On `pace-0`, the old route spent 10.52 and 5.53 days on the prerequisite clears, then 3.52 and 3.26 days on the two legs. The River Tire’s history was memorable enough to preserve mechanically. Ordinary carryover already preserves its physical identity, condition and origin. Adding a Dare or perk would still prescribe an itinerary to unlock a single payment option.

Removed the qualifying and delivery proofs, permit counts, part safeguards and release dialogs, build and race tags, away reports and abandonment confirmations. Existing saves decode through the normal schema. It discards retired progress and guard fields while retaining parts, ordinary Keep flags, challenge records and previously paid Season freight. No channel, income source, new save field or first-Season unlock was added.

## Owner playtest
