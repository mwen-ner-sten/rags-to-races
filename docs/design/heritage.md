# Heritage and the Hall of Fame

## Decision

Keep Heritage experimental. Give each model one permanent display in the Hall of Fame and let the player decide which racer deserves it. The ordinary archive stays open to everyone. Mower dismantling and exchange still serve the immediate garage economy.

A retired car keeps its name, race record and fitted parts. After a reset, an opt-in player can pick a previous Season’s complete, race-finished build in Workshop → Heritage. Recreating it faithfully costs the shakedown car and its installed parts. Changing the build while retaining an original component model costs a Feature podium at the model’s home venue, and the racer stays in the garage. Both routes dedicate the model’s display. The Notebook shows the original and tribute names, model, route, sprite and dedication Season.

## Cuts and compatibility

Removed the repeat drawing and jig purchases and the ability to overwrite a display with a newly started project. Kept both endings because surrendering an assembled car and proving an altered build on track are materially different choices. Returning a physical car at Season start would also carry its parts across the reset and compete with opening perks. Rebuilding from the archived specification preserves the connection to scavenging.

Existing saves use the same schema. Older dual-route awards remain visible, open legacy projects can finish, and a jig already paid for lasts through its Season. Plans already acquired follow ordinary knowledge rules. New projects cannot displace a dedicated model. Removed the archive’s oldest-entry eviction at 200 cars so a long-running player can keep the first mower. Records already evicted by older versions cannot be recovered. Long-lived archives will increase save size.

## Play evidence and owner questions

Four mixed-schedule seeds rebuilt a Push Mower on each route using public actions. All eight earned a display, reached the next Scrap Reset, and loaded with the model still dedicated. Donation took 0.53–0.92 simulated days after starting the Season. Podiums took 0.92–1.76. These are mower results. The longitudinal career cohort exercised resets but did not measure attachment to these cars.

The full unit suite passed 365 tests, including dedication across both reset layers, importing an existing jig and retaining the 201st archive entry. Six browser cases passed across desktop and 390px mobile for Heritage, mower exchange and the offline museum shakedown.

The four-seed pacing check produced identical output before and after. Daily, mixed and active first-Season medians were 20.4, 6.4 and 5.3 days. Five of seven targets passed. The slowest daily seed took 21.4 days against a 21-day ceiling, and the mixed median fell below the 6.5-day floor.

- After earning one display, is there another car you want to honour without a mechanical reward?
- Try a higher-tier special. Does hunting for a podium feel like bringing back a favourite, or replaying an event for a label?
