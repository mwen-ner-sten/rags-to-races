# Grassroots Clubs

Two different Circuit certificates open the Club Office. A club is an optional charter for a new Scrap Season. The normal County Fair Feature reset gate still applies. Players choose a club, their regular Season objective and perks, and any earned donor carryover in the same planner.

| Charter | Opening support | Calendar | Certification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Backyard Co-op | Worn Lawn Mower Engine and Basic Wheels | Push Mower Sprint and Feature in the Backyard, followed by a Riding Mower Sprint and Feature at Dirt | 17 points, three finishes, two free-gate finishes. Pays 55 Scrap Bucks and 5 Rep. |
| Fairground Guild | 120 Scrap Bucks, three metal and two rubber | A Go-Kart card alternating between Dirt and the County Fair | 23 points, three finishes, one free-gate finish. Pays 150 Scrap Bucks and 8 Rep. |

The Club Office signs up the player’s actual car through the main race queue. The event uses its ordinary ladder, prerequisites, wear, schedule, and entry charge. Before each entry the player decides whether the race is ticketed or open to the public. Ticketed podiums pay an additional five Scrap Bucks or the round’s free-gate cost, whichever is higher. Free gates add a round-specific hosting fee and award two Rep on a classified finish. They also satisfy the public requirement. Fees are charged when the race starts. A waiting sign-up can be withdrawn using the queue, and the ordinary race results still apply when the race finishes.

The next Scrap Reset archives the card, including incomplete attempts. It keeps the team, crew, Owner history, Circuit certificates and past club cards. Each new Season clears the main garage, cash, Rep and venues under the usual rules. An earned donor can occupy its normal perk slot independently of the club choice. The club’s completion grant goes into the current garage economy and can fund the rest of that Season. Players can pick either club again or run a non-club Season while retaining their archive.

## Track founding

Certify either club card to open a new layout choice on future Club resets. Each layout modifies its official Club races only. The ordinary venue race, Owner calendar, and Circuit Office keep their standard road or oval. A Club can certify under the standard layout, and either club charter can choose either custom layout on repeat Seasons.

| Layout | Race effect | License target | Next-season workshop loan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tight Infield | Weights handling and traction more heavily, reduces wear and failure risk, and puts switchbacks and gravel into Rally. Drag favors traction off the line. No extra entry cost. | Certify the club with two Nurse podiums | Good Knobby Tires |
| Fast Straight | Weights power more heavily, raises wear and failure risk, and adds Rally fast lanes. Costs three additional Scrap Bucks per start. | Certify the club with two Push podiums | Good Tiller Engine |

The layout, odds, sealed race result, HUD label, and replay all use the same course profile. Track licenses survive future resets and appear in the club archive. After a license is earned, the next Club plan can borrow one Good part from any licensed layout, independent of the active course. This can give an opposite-layout workshop a viable new setup without consuming the ordinary donor slot. The part lasts for that Season, uses its normal condition and fit rules, and bears its workshop provenance. Discarding it does not create a replacement. A later Club reset can take another single loan. Independent and Collective team organizations keep their existing rules.

## Visiting workshops

Moving to River or Quarry County also opens a single local Sprint matchup. Enter with a Push Mower carrying a part hauled locally in the current Season. A Curbside find can qualify, so the player can build a contender before opening the county’s specialist source. The match runs as a real Sprint at the area's normal Backyard venue and uses the ordinary cooldown, wear, practice and race queue. A loss, breakdown, or rival retirement can be retried after repairs or a new setup. The contact is earned once by finishing ahead of the rival while their car is still running.

| Contact | Racing profile | Future Club workshop loan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inez Marsh, River County | Steady pace and low breakdown risk | A Good River Tire from Floodplain Lots |
| Wade Slater, Quarry County | Higher pace and greater variation, with more breakdown risk | A Good Quarry Four from Quarry Breakers |

An earned contact can supply the next Club even if it is founded in another county. Visitor loans share the one-part workshop loan slot with track licenses. The physical part keeps the visitor’s name, workshop, county and the Season in which the relationship was earned. Garage and Hall of Fame views display that provenance. A borrowed part from another county does not count as a fresh local haul.
