# Grassroots Clubs

Two different Circuit certificates open the Club Office. A club is an optional charter for a new Scrap Season. The normal County Fair Feature reset gate still applies. Players choose a club, their regular Season objective and perks, and any earned donor carryover in the same planner.

| Charter | Opening support | Calendar | Certification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Backyard Co-op | Worn Lawn Mower Engine and Basic Wheels | Push Mower Sprint and Feature in the Backyard, followed by a Riding Mower Sprint and Feature at Dirt | 17 points, three finishes, two free-gate finishes. Pays 55 Scrap Bucks and 5 Rep. |
| Fairground Guild | 120 Scrap Bucks, three metal and two rubber | A Go-Kart card alternating between Dirt and the County Fair | 23 points, three finishes, one free-gate finish. Pays 150 Scrap Bucks and 8 Rep. |

The Club Office signs up the player’s actual car through the main race queue. The event uses its ordinary ladder, prerequisites, wear, schedule, and entry charge. Before each entry the player decides whether the race is ticketed or open to the public. Ticketed podiums pay an additional five Scrap Bucks or the round’s free-gate cost, whichever is higher. Free gates add a round-specific hosting fee and award two Rep on a classified finish. They also satisfy the public requirement. Fees are charged when the race starts. A waiting sign-up can be withdrawn using the queue, and the ordinary race results still apply when the race finishes.

The next Scrap Reset archives the card, including incomplete attempts. It keeps the team, crew, Owner history, Circuit certificates and past club cards. Each new Season clears the main garage, cash, Rep and venues under the usual rules. An earned donor can occupy its normal perk slot independently of the club choice. The club’s completion grant goes into the current garage economy and can fund the rest of that Season. Players can pick either club again or run a non-club Season while retaining their archive.
