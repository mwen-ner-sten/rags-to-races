# Builder iterations 1–13

These changes follow the player from choosing a haul through fitting parts, scheduling workshop work, and planning the next Season. The numbered Vitest cases in `src/core/__tests__/builder-iterations.test.ts` check the same helpers or actions used by the game. The challenge and career tests exercise the surrounding reset and save behavior.

| Iteration | Player-facing change | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scouting now shows the chance of each find and updates the odds as you change the part category. | The weights sum to one, Scouting boosts the chosen category, and the random haul uses the same weights as the preview. |
| 2 | A blueprint with a missing required part lists open yards that carry compatible parts, ranked by the chance of a match per random find. | A missing kart chassis points to Marisol's Junkyard before the Neighbourhood Yards. Curbside, closed yards, and the incompatible Auction pool stay out of the results. |
| 3 | Keep protects a part from automatic driveway sorting, replacement during a driveway swap, and the Sell All Junk shortcut. | The mark survives save decoding, excludes the part from automatic swap selection, and protects it under all four overflow rules. Deliberate single-part sales and stripping are available. |
| 4 | The vehicle's swap list shows each candidate's score change for the selected track, current tune, and the part it would replace. The part's origin is visible before choosing it. | The preview matches the actual score change after installing the replacement and changes when the vehicle is retuned. |
| 5 | Queue cleaning for up to three parts in the current garage view. The full batch is accepted together. | Both eligible parts eventually improve. A batch containing an ineligible part leaves all parts and jobs untouched. |
| 6 | Pull and service an installed part from the vehicle card. The garage needs room, and a signed-up or running race reserves the vehicle. | The engine moves once from the car to the bench and stays reserved there. A race sign-up blocks the operation. |
| 7 | Blueprints show fitted required-part counts, actual cash and material deficits, and compatible parts currently needing bench work. | A scrapped chassis is listed for bench work, exact material deficits are computed, and a stale part selection falls back to a valid available part. |
| 8 | The work board can promote a queued workshop job without interrupting current work or reordering race sign-ups. | A queued cleaning job moves ahead, the active bench job stays put, and the promoted job starts next. |
| 9 | Reset planning offers three editable presets for salvage, workshop, and kart-first Seasons. They assign available tune-up points and use regular perk slots and costs. | Routes select distinct skills, enforce the slot and LP limits, and block the kart loadout until its perk is earned. |
| 10 | The Garage and Legacy panels show whether the current build qualifies for its next challenge checkpoint and what to change if it does not. | Incorrect tires block both the preview and credit. The shared check accepts the correct Dirt setup and then asks for Fair tires on the same kart and frame. |
| 11 | Hand-Me-Down Horsepower challenges the player to move the exact Dirt-winning mower engine to a Fair-winning kart. It earns capped Assembly mastery. | Save decoding retains the winning engine's identity. A similar or better engine earns no credit, then a physical transfer and repeat Fair win complete the challenge. With a Restored Four-Cylinder, appropriate support parts and full practice, Dirt Oval estimates were 93% for the mower at Dirt and 69% for the kart at the Fair. |
| 12 | Three Parts With a Past clears unlock the Auction Bidder Card. It opens an early source of parts at zero Rep, while every trip costs 40 Scrap Bucks. | After three challenge settlements, the test resets with zero Rep and zero cash, sells real curb finds, pays the fee, and receives auction loot. One Yard allows only the auction once chosen. A browser regression checks the early card and paid entry blocker. |
| 13 | A Team Reset can spend the ending Season's earned LP on perks. The planner shows exactly what unspent LP will expire as selections change. | A normal Team Reset spends earnings that exceed the previous balance, credits lifetime LP once, and starts the new Season with zero LP. A browser regression checks that the displayed forfeiture falls when a perk is purchased. |

The grouped builder, challenges, core, and depth run passed 87 tests, including all 13 numbered cases. The combined Vitest run passed 237 tests. TypeScript and targeted ESLint checks passed. Browser cases for protection and batch cleaning, early auctions, and the Team Reset budget are in `tests/playwright/builder-iterations.spec.ts`.
