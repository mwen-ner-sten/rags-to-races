# Backyard and Dirt mower retirement

After a Push Mower or Riding Mower finishes a race, the Garage offers two ways to retire it. Dismantling keeps one fitted part with its current condition. The other parts yield the listed materials. Exchanging takes the complete mower and 12 Scrap Bucks in return for a Worn Tiller Engine and Worn Knobby Tire. Both components fit the free Push Mower plans. The kit can also supply an engine and tires for another Riding Mower once its frame and build costs are ready.

The Garage previews capacity, Junkyard access and the extra Habit slot when retiring the last Riding Mower affects them. The mower must come back from its race and clear any sign-ups first. The exchange needs room for both kit pieces. A retirement leaves the established event ladders, knowledge, reputation and complete race results in place.

## Paired opening simulation

Eight paired seeds start with the same assembled Push Mower, its first completed Backyard Sprint, and enough proceeds from the same part sale and race to afford the exchange. The dismantling policy keeps the Lawn Mower Engine and hauls until it has a usable replacement tire, selling spares if storage fills. Both paths use the normal assembly job for the replacement.

| Retirement | Median return to first replacement | Curbside trips | Median cash cost | Median rubber gained | Replacement handling | Replacement reliability |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dismantle and keep the Lawn Mower Engine | 88.6 seconds | 2 | 0 | 2 | 4.6 | 16.4 |
| Exchange for a pre-matched kit | 60 seconds | 0 | 12 | 0 | 5.4 | 28 |

Dismantling preserved the higher-powered engine and supplied rubber for later bench work. The exchange paid for an immediate, dependable reliability setup with more handling and a lower power score. Results cover a Push Mower returning to the Backyard and depend on the selected build and eight seeds.

The core suite exercises actual race entry, retirement, and replacement assembly. It also checks the payment and capacity blockers, repeated use, original archived build and result, stale race Habit cleanup, and save validation.
