# Owner gameplay iterations

Verified in the Windows development checkout with the normal game action, race queue, settlement, and save decoder where applicable. The focused checks use `npx vitest run src/core/__tests__/ownership.test.ts -t "<test name>"`.

| Iteration | Playable change | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 14 | Entry suggestions rank legal cars by readiness and the current race forecast. The entry screen shows win odds and DNF risk together. | The Fair excludes Street Stock. Regional recommends it over the earlier-built Beater, then recommends the Beater when the Stock's engine comes out. |
| 15 | An ordinary sign-up occupying the same slot is called out before trying to submit. Queued entries explain preparation blockers while retaining withdrawal. | A normal queued Fair entry blocks a championship duplicate. A championship entry waiting through a cooldown displays its exact entry fee when the team runs out of cash. |
| 16 | The calendar shows what remains mathematically possible, including when a skip would end the title bid or the title is already secured. | Two early skips leave 36 points attainable and a third reduces the ceiling to 24. |
| 17 | Settle and display the four counting results and the dropped result from the same score calculation. A dropped scoring car still fulfills the Works lineup rule. | Four wins drop one fourth-place result from the points, and that result still gives the Works team two scoring cars. |
| 18 | Personal bests and title counts are banked by charter and discipline. A weak rematch and the recent-history cap do not overwrite a career best. | A normal Dirt title saves its score. Loading an older save recovers the retained Dirt record; a zero-point replay leaves it intact. |
| 19 | A title unlocks optional sponsor contracts. The player chooses a stake and performance target before entering the next tour. Self-funding stays available. | A retired non-title team cannot sign. A title team cannot offer a 450 Scrap Buck stake with 449 in the bank; the legal stake is charged once and survives reloading. |
| 20 | Reliable Parts pays for a score in all five rounds. Front Row Fuel pays more for three wins. Sponsor progress settles alongside race results and skipped rounds. | Replay the same actual tour from one seed with each contract and without one. The successful variants earn exactly 450 and 1,100 extra Scrap Bucks. A skip can lose the consistency stake even on a championship run. |
| 21 | The settled race budget separates paid entry fees, race winnings, title purse, and sponsor settlement. | A sponsored title with 790 Scrap Bucks in actual fees reconciles to the wallet delta. An older race slip without recorded fees omits the incomplete total. |
| 22 | A title in the current charter and discipline opens the reverse calendar. Owners can tackle a late event first and rearrange garage preparation. | An unproven team cannot select reverse. A winning team signs up for State first, saves during its cooldown, and scores the correct State race after reloading. |
| 23 | Win the same charter in Dirt Oval and Drag to open an all-Feature calendar. Heats become longer races with Feature fields and cooldowns. | A Dirt title alone cannot open the route. An actual Team Reset into Drag and a second title open it. The first two positions run actual Fair and Regional Features. |
| 24 | Expand archived results to compare the actual tires, engine, tune, and pace used for each round. | The saved Fair slip retains the Beater’s V6, Sport Tires, power tune, and Nurse call after a full Scrap Reset removes the car. |
| 25 | A last-chance Works round recommends a ready reserve ahead of an already-scoring lead car. Historical round IDs are unique in the save ledger. | Four scoring Stock rounds survive a reload, then the suggested Beater secures the title in the fifth. A save duplicating the race across round slips is rejected. |
