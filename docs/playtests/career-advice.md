# Returning career playtest

The normal action-driven push bot reached its earned first Team Reset in Season 2 at day 17.3 on `pace-0` and day 18.3 on `pace-1` with four 15-minute visits per day. Both had won the State Invitational Feature and had no qualifying Season challenge. On those saves, Stage Rally was locked at first founding. Founding normally unlocked it for a later Team Reset and exposed a new Team-era State requirement for the touring licence.

The Career opportunities card now appears on return once the player approaches Legacy or Team. It compares both direct first-founding Rally routes with ordinary founding and names the car from a completed challenge. In Team and Owner it points to the current qualifying race, choices in the next Team Reset, an eligible crew handover, or the next touring round. A link opens and focuses the underlying control. The player still reviews and confirms the reset or entry.

The normal queue also verified that a reverse tour's away report names the race that actually ran and the next event in the chosen order. It previously reported the standard calendar.

The focused unit suite covers older Builder saves, Team-only touring proof, crew handover advice, action-driven Owner round settlement, and the away report. The browser suite covers objective drafting from an old save, the Stage Rally radio and confirmation gate, and jumping to Owner round controls.
