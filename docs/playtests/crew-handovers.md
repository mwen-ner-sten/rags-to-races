# Crew handovers playtest

A Team Reset may spend 8 Team Points on one handover from a departing member. The offer needs crew level 2 and recorded service. Old Hands can keep a different member. Older saves load with their existing experience totals, and newly completed jobs fill in specific service.

| Route from the same old roster | Handover qualification | Next team |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workshop apprenticeship | Rosa completes six qualifying cleaning jobs. | Hire a successor and have that person complete six matching jobs. Player bench work cannot advance it. Dismissing an unfinished apprentice restarts training. Graduation teaches the Habit across Scrap Resets for this team and releases the crew member from training. |
| Track notes | DeShawn is credited for driving in three real Dirt Track Sprints. | Open the venue normally in the same discipline. The Heat is now available without a Sprint podium. The normal Heat results still determine Feature qualification. |
| No handover | Same roster and same reset. | Keep the 8 Team Points and train or qualify through the ordinary rules. |

The tests fork one prepared team through the regular Team Reset flow. They verify the charge, reject a simultaneous Old Hands retention of the mentor, play the practice jobs to graduation, and confirm that the inherited notes open only the targeted Heat. The original crew members are not hired by either handover. Save checks cover old roster data, an apprenticeship in progress, and a graduated apprentice who has left the team.

Verified with `npx vitest run src/core/__tests__/crew-legacy.test.ts`.
